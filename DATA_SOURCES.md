Files such as `sleep_analysis_merged.csv`, `wrist_temp_analysis.csv`, `phase_lengths.csv`, `kruskal_results_sleep.csv` and `spearman_sleep_hormones.csv` are analysis outputs, not required source datasets. The public repository excludes derived participant records and saved menstrual-health outputs.

The surrounding Jupyter workspace also contains exercises, images and other datasets. They have not been added merely because they are in the same course folders. The MEG/fMRI project remains outside this repository until its source files are available from the Linux environment.

Source links and licence details are recorded in each project's README. Prepared notebook copies may contain documented fixes; the original Jupyter coursework was left unchanged.
